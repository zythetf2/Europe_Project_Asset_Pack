===========
EUROPA GAMEPLAY SIGNS
===========

By Goobert (https://steamcommunity.com/id/largedrink89) and Entropy Tempus (https://steamcommunity.com/id/entropy_tempus/)


This upgraded signs have been translated accurately to different languages from english. Even tho not all translations are exactly the same they are close enough in context for them to work

Battlements - Skin0, Skin1
Control Point - Skin2, Skin3
Resupply - Skin4, Skin5
Intelligence - Skin6, Skin7
Exit - Skin8, Skin9
Capture Point - Skin10, Skin11
Off blu - Skin12
Off red - Skin13
Off neutral - Skin14

They will have the same name as their original counterparts just with the language they use added to the end. The original counterparts are
